MaQasa Real Estate · Brand Strategy · Social Media · SEO · Paid Media

From an unknown local agency to a recognised real estate brand.

How strategy, brand communication, organic visibility and paid acquisition came together to build awareness and generate opportunities.

The Challenge

From being unknown to becoming a local reference.

MaQasa was a local real estate agency in a small town in Valencia. The challenge was clear: increase awareness, attract homeowners who were considering selling and help clients sell their properties.

The problem wasn't simply visibility. The brand needed to become recognisable, relevant and trustworthy enough for people to consider it when an important decision came up.

The Thinking

Before asking people to choose MaQasa, we needed to give them a reason to remember it.

The strategy started with the idea that awareness and acquisition couldn't be treated as separate problems. The brand needed to become visible first, then that visibility needed to connect naturally with useful information, trust and opportunities to get in touch.

The Strategy

Build the brand. Build visibility. Create opportunities.

  1. 01

    Brand

    Define a clearer visual and communication style so MaQasa could build recognition and communicate consistently.

  2. 02

    Social Media

    Use social media to increase awareness, communicate the brand and become part of the local conversation.

  3. 03

    SEO

    Build organic visibility through the website, including informational content and content specifically designed to attract potential leads.

  4. 04

    Paid Media

    Use Meta Ads and Google Ads to reach relevant audiences and support acquisition.

  5. 05

    Connection

    Connect the attention generated through social, search and advertising with the website and content so visibility could move towards conversion.

The Execution

Different channels. One direction.

Branding

  • Visual identity
  • Communication style
  • Positioning

Social Media

  • Content
  • Awareness
  • Community
  • Brand communication

SEO

  • Informational content
  • Search visibility
  • Lead-focused content

Paid Media

  • Meta Ads
  • Google Ads
  • Acquisition

The Result

A stronger position in the local market.

The combination of brand building, organic visibility, paid acquisition and consistent communication helped position MaQasa as a recognised real estate agency in its local area of Valencia.

The Takeaway

Marketing works better when every action knows what it's there to do.

The value wasn't in having more channels. It was in making each one contribute to the same business objective.
