About Marina

Behind the strategy, there's a curious mind.

I'm Marina — a marketing consultant who believes the best ideas happen where creativity, strategy and data meet.

My Story

Curiosity has always been part of the way I work.

Over the past five years, I've learned that marketing is much more than strategies and campaigns. It's about people, ideas and stories and helping them shine in their own way.

I've always felt that my essence was to bring out the best in the people and brands I work with. Because I believe a brand stands out through the people behind it the ones who make it visible, connect with others and give it meaning.

After five years in marketing, I'm now ready for a new chapter: stepping outside my comfort zone, embracing new cultures and perspectives, and continuing to learn.

That curiosity is one of the things I bring into every project.

How I Think

Three things shape the way I approach marketing.

  1. 01

    Curious

    I ask questions before I look for answers. Understanding the business, the audience and the context comes first.

  2. 02

    Strategic

    I look at the bigger picture. Every action should have a reason, and every channel should have a role.

  3. 03

    Creative

    I believe strategy becomes more powerful when it gives people something worth noticing, remembering and talking about.
