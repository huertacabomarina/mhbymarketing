2. Website hosting

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). To deliver the pages and protect them against attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit. This data is not used by me to identify you.

3. Google Analytics — only with your consent

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA) to understand how visitors use it. Google Analytics is only loaded after you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. If you decline, or until you choose, no Google Analytics code is loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

When you accept, Google Analytics collects information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, your approximate location (country/city), your device and browser, and certain interactions — clicks on the email address or the call booking link, and successful contact form submissions. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_E90RZC0LZ3, which last up to two years.

You can change your choice at any time via (also in the footer of every page). If you withdraw your consent, Google Analytics stops loading and its cookies are removed from your browser.

If you use the contact form, the data you enter — name, email address, company or website, the service you're interested in, your message and when you'd like to start — is sent via Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA) and delivered to my email inbox. I use it only to answer your enquiry and, if we work together, to prepare that collaboration.

5. Email

If you write to me by email, and when contact form messages arrive, they are stored in my Gmail account (Google). I keep them only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting collaboration, and as required by statutory retention periods.

6. Booking a call

Discovery calls are booked through a Google Calendar appointment page and held on Google Meet (Google LLC). Google's own privacy policy applies to these services.

10. Your rights

Under the nFADP you have the right to:

  • know whether and which personal data about you is processed (right of access);
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted or its processing restricted;
  • object to processing, and withdraw any consent you've given, with effect for the future;
  • receive the data you provided in a common electronic format (data portability).

To exercise any of these rights, write to huertacabomarina@gmail.com. You may be asked for proof of identity. You can also lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), edoeb.admin.ch.

If you are in the European Union, the GDPR may also apply. In that case, analytics is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and answering your enquiries on steps prior to a contract and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
