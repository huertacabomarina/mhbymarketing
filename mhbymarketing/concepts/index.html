How Concepts Work

Real strategy. Fictional brands.

Each concept starts with an invented business — a name, an audience, a market — and works through how I'd approach its marketing from the ground up: brand strategy, content and social media, SEO, paid media, or whichever mix that business actually needs.

These are self-directed exercises, not real client work. There are no real results, figures or testimonials here — just the strategic thinking behind a brand, made visible.
